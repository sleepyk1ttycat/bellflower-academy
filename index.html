The Mystery of Bellflower

Bellflower Academy seems like an ordinary school. Its students follow their routines, attend classes and pass through familiar hallways.

Yet the old west wing remains forbidden. Strange sounds are sometimes heard behind its doors, and nobody seems willing to explain why.

On the first day of a new school year, the bell rings thirteen times. Soon after, an old photograph is found. Eleven students appear in it, alongside a mysterious note: "One of you already knows what happened."

Twenty years of forgotten history are about to come back to the surface.
